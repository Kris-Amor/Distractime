Distractime

How this works

Tell it how long a video is and where you are in it. It shows how much is left, and splits your real time into watching, distraction and pauses.

Two ways to use it

Manual: type your current time and the runtime, then press Calculate. Each time you press it again, the page compares your new time with your last entry. Video time you moved through counts as watched, and the rest of the real time counts as distraction.

Auto: type the runtime (and where you are), then press Start. A clock follows along. Use Pause when you step away on purpose, and that time is counted separately, not as distraction. You can still press Calculate to type where you really are, and the page works out the difference.

Everything stays in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Real Time Elapsed updates every second while the clock runs. The other totals update when you press Calculate. Paused updates when you resume or stop.

What the words mean

Time Remaining
Runtime minus your current position in the video.
Current Time
Where you are in the video. While the clock runs, it follows the clock.
Runtime
The total length of the video.
Total Time
All real time counted so far, across sessions. It updates when you press Calculate.
Distraction
Real time spent not paused, minus how far the video position moved. It can read negative if you skip ahead faster than the clock.
Real Time Elapsed
Real time counted this session. It resets when you refresh.
Watched
How far your video position moved between entries. A rewind counts the same as moving forward.
Paused
Real time spent paused with the Pause button. It is added when you Resume or Stop.
